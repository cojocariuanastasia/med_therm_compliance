The client is interested in exploring whether AI can be used to help analyze operational data from a medical device. They want to understand if it is possible to automatically check device logs, simple charts, and technical documents against a set of predefined rules or guidelines. The goal is to identify potential issues, inconsistencies, or unusual behavior in the data and present them in a clear and understandable way. The solution should demonstrate basic capabilities of extracting information from text and visual inputs and comparing it against simple rule-based conditions.
