The client is requesting a Proof of Concept for an AI-driven compliance and operational intelligence platform for regulated medical devices, specifically portable temperature-controlled plasma transport units.

The objective is to evaluate the feasibility of using multimodal large language models (VLLMs) to ingest, interpret, and validate heterogeneous data sources including regulatory documentation, engineering specifications, time-series sensor logs, alarm/event streams, operational telemetry, system dashboards, and blueprint or schematic images.

The system should be capable of:

extracting structured regulatory requirements from dense compliance documents
mapping technical specifications against regulatory constraints and identifying gaps or contradictions
analyzing time-series data (e.g., temperature, battery, sensor readings) from line and scatter plots to detect threshold violations, anomalies, and instability patterns
interpreting system logs and event streams to reconstruct operational behavior and detect compliance violations, including delayed alarms, missing sensor redundancy, or telemetry gaps
understanding engineering blueprints or system schematics to validate physical layout constraints such as sensor placement, airflow design, insulation requirements, and redundancy architecture
correlating findings across multiple modalities (logs, charts, specifications, and diagrams) to produce root cause analysis and explainable compliance conclusions

The expected output should include:

a structured compliance report aligned with regulatory requirements
identification of deviations, missing constraints, and ambiguous specifications
temporal analysis of system behavior and event sequences
explanation of detected violations with supporting evidence from logs and visual data
a severity classification of issues based on regulatory impact

The PoC should demonstrate how AI can function as an assistive compliance analyst capable of supporting engineering, quality assurance, and regulatory teams in high-stakes medical device environments.
